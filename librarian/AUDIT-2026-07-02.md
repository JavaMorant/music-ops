## Why you saw inconsistencies — the five root causes

1. **Run backups live INSIDE the library and every scanner counts them.** `~/DJ/library/.librarian/runs/…/backup/`
   holds 443 audio copies in genre-named folders; `paths.audio_files()` skips only `_quarantine`, so every
   scan/plan/count includes them, and a dedup tie prefers the hidden backup over your live copy. (C1)
2. **Two engines with two taxonomies both ran on the same library.** Folders follow the new organise buckets,
   genre *tags* still carry the retired leaf taxonomy, three genre vocabularies coexist in code, and two
   contradictory filename policies ship at once — so counts, names, and genres disagree depending on which
   path you look through. (C-family + U-findings)
3. **What you reviewed is not what was applied.** `organise --apply` recomputes the whole pipeline instead of
   executing the reviewed plan JSON — the real 29-Jun run matches neither saved plan (12 reviewed moves
   dropped, 5 unreviewed executed). (C7)
4. **rekordbox cue safety was effectively off.** All 52 applied runs ran with `rekordbox: null` (7,738 files
   moved), 98% of the 40 library .m3u playlist entries now point at dead paths, and USB-play sync matches by
   title only while writing into live master.db. (C2, U17–U24)
5. **Runs/journals/quarantine are fragmented.** Journals sit in three locations (`librarian runs`/`undo`
   default to a cwd-relative dir containing none of them), quarantine spans four locations, and 44 applied
   runs were journaled with `library_root=/Users/awandedibidi` (your whole home dir) and `backup:false`. (C6, C10, U49)

### L1 [MEDIUM] preflight does not reject two actions with the same source, so a plan that passes preflight is guaranteed to abort mid-apply — contradicting its all-or-nothing contract

- **Where:** `src/librarian/engine.py` · found by: safety-core
- **Evidence:** preflight checks src.exists(), containment, dest uniqueness and dest-free, but never collects sources. Demonstrated: a Plan with two MOVE actions from the same src passed preflight; apply executed action 1 then raised 'source missing, refusing to move' on action 2, leaving journal ['done','pending'], status 'applying'. Docstring promises 'all-or-nothing-safe apply'.
- **Why low impact:** Unreachable and harmless in this user's setup.
